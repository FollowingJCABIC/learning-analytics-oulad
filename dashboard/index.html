Limits

Clicks are traces, not explanations

OULAD describes selected anonymized Open University modules from 2013-2014. Clicks do not directly measure effort, motivation, attention, or learning. Withdrawal may reflect circumstances absent from the data. Findings are associations and may not generalize to another institution or period.
